# 01 — Product: what TradeMatrix AI is and is not

*Created by Argy*

## One-sentence product

TradeMatrix AI tells a crypto trader how likely a coin is to close higher or lower on its next 1h, 4h or 1d candle, shows the reasons, and proves how past signals actually performed.

## Users

- **Primary:** retail crypto traders who watch charts on web and phone and want a second opinion backed by data.
- **First user:** Argy and friends in a private beta. A public launch needs the legal check in docs/06.

## Brand and design

- Name: **TradeMatrix AI**. Under the name, in smaller muted text: **Created by Argy**.
- Where the brand line appears: web header/logo, web footer, Flutter splash screen, About page on both apps.
- Dark theme by default, light theme available. Calm, data-first look, no hype language ("guaranteed", "sure win", "100%" are forbidden in all copy).
- Up = green, Down = red, Neutral = grey/amber. Never rely on color alone: always add an icon/arrow and a text label (accessibility).
- Default timezone for display: Asia/Jakarta (WIB). Currency display: USDT price, with an optional IDR conversion later (not v1).

## v1 scope

**Coins (vs USDT):** BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, plus (Argy's scope change, 2026-10-03) DOGE, ADA, LINK, AVAX, UNI, NEAR, LTC, TRX, DOT, BCH, XLM: 16 coins. Rule for adding more: about 6+ years of Binance history and solid daily volume. Stored in the `assets` table so more can be added without code changes (one migration + backfill + model training).
**Timeframes:** 1h, 4h, 1d. One prediction per coin per timeframe per candle close (the next candle).

### Must have

1. **Market list** with each coin's last price, 24h change and current signal chip.
2. **Coin detail page** with:
   - Live candlestick chart (timeframe switcher 1h / 4h / 1d) and volume.
   - Indicator overlays that can be toggled: EMA 9/21/50, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD (data comes from the API).
   - **Signal card** (see below).
   - News list with a sentiment badge per headline.
3. **Track-record page** (public): accuracy, baseline accuracy, Brier score, number of signals, coverage (share of non-neutral signals), for the last 30 / 90 days, per coin and timeframe, plus a list of recent signals with their outcomes.
4. **Auth:** email + password and Google sign-in through Supabase Auth, on web and mobile.
5. **Watchlist** (login required).
6. **Alerts** (login required): signal change, probability above/below a threshold, price above/below. Delivered as in-app notifications and as push notifications on mobile.
7. **About page** with how the signal works in plain language, the baseline explanation, the disclaimer and the brand line.

### Should have

- Light/dark toggle, localized number/date formats, empty/error/loading states everywhere.
- Email delivery of alerts on web (Resend or similar) — only after core alerts work.

### Won't have in v1

Placing trades, exchange API keys, futures/leverage, stocks/forex, paid plans/payments, social features, copy trading, price targets ("it will reach $X").

## Signal card (the core UI object)

Shows, for one coin and timeframe:

- Label: **Up**, **Down** or **Neutral** (icon + text).
- Probability of Up as a percentage with one decimal (e.g. `58.2% up`). For Down show `100 − p`.
- Neutral band: if `p_up` is between 45% and 55% the label is Neutral (configurable constant, see docs/03).
- "Valid for": the target candle (e.g. `Next 1h candle, closes 14:00 WIB`) and a countdown.
- **Why** (top 3 reasons), generated by templates from the feature snapshot, for example: `RSI 71 (overbought)`, `EMA 9 crossed below EMA 21`, `News sentiment +0.31 (ETF approval headlines)`. Reasons are deterministic templates, not free-form LLM text.
- Model confidence quality: the recent hit rate of this model on this coin/timeframe (from the track record) next to the baseline.
- Disclaimer line (text in docs/06).

## Notifications

- Alert types: `signal_change` (label changed vs the previous candle), `prob_above`, `prob_below`, `price_above`, `price_below`.
- An alert fires at most once per candle per rule (no spam). A `cooldown_minutes` field exists on every rule.
- Mobile gets FCM push; both clients show the in-app notification list.

## Quality bar

- Chart updates within about 1 second of a live candle update.
- A new prediction appears in the apps within about 30 seconds after a candle closes.
- Pages are usable on a 360 px wide phone and on desktop.
- Accessible: keyboard navigation on web, labels on icons, contrast AA.

## Success criteria for the MVP

- All five coins × three timeframes produce a stored prediction at every candle close for 14 days without a missed run.
- The track-record page shows live numbers that match a manual SQL check.
- The model's out-of-sample directional accuracy is reported honestly next to the naive baseline, whatever the result.
- Argy can explain every part of the system.
